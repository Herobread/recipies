## Ingredients

- <span data-qty="500">500</span>g oat milk
- <span data-qty="50">50</span>g fresh yeast (or <span data-qty="14">14</span>g dry yeast)
- <span data-qty="150">150</span>g butter - softened, for the dough
- <span data-qty="90">90</span>g caster sugar - for the dough
- <span data-qty="1">1</span>tsp salt
- <span data-qty="1">1</span>tbsp cardamom seeds or ground cardamom
- <span data-qty="780">780</span>g wheat flour
- <span data-qty="150">150</span>g butter - softened, for the filling
- <span data-qty="90">90</span>g caster sugar - for the filling
- <span data-qty="2">2</span>tbsp ground cinnamon
- <span data-qty="1">1</span>egg - for egg wash
- Pearl sugar or raw sugar - for topping

## Method

1. **Heat oat milk:** Heat the oat milk to 37°C (98°F).

2. **Dissolve yeast:** In a large bowl, crumble the yeast (or add dry yeast) and pour in the warm oat milk. Stir until dissolved.

3. **Knead dough:** Add the butter, sugar, salt, cardamom, and flour. Work the dough for about 10 minutes in a stand mixer until smooth, elastic, and just firm enough to touch without sticking to your fingers.

4. **First rise:** Cover the bowl and let the dough rise for about 30 minutes, or until doubled in size.

5. **Make filling:** Combine the softened butter, sugar, and cinnamon in a bowl until smooth and spreadable.

6. **Roll out:** Turn the dough onto a lightly floured surface and knead lightly, adding additional flour as necessary until it just loses its stickiness. Roll into a large rectangle, about 1 cm (½-inch) thick.

7. **Fill & fold:** Spread the filling evenly over the dough. Fold the dough in half once.

8. **Cut & shape:** With a pizza cutter, cut the dough into <span data-qty="20">20</span>slices about 2 cm (1 inch) wide. Twist each strip of dough and tie into a knot, tucking the end underneath the bun.

9. **Arrange:** Place the buns on baking sheets lined with baking paper.

10. **Second rise:** Cover with a towel and let rise for 30 minutes. Meanwhile, preheat the oven to 250°C (480°F).

11. **Egg wash & top:** Whisk the egg in a small bowl, brush over the buns, and sprinkle generously with pearl sugar or raw sugar.

12. **Bake:** Bake in the middle of the oven for 6-8 minutes until golden brown on both top and bottom.

13. **Cool:** Transfer the buns to a wire rack and let cool completely.
