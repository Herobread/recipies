## Ingredients

- <span data-qty="2">2</span> duck breasts
- **Fine salt** - for dry brining
- <span data-qty="2">2</span> tbsp hoisin sauce
- <span data-qty="1">1</span> tsp soy sauce
- <span data-qty="1">1</span> tsp rice vinegar
- <span data-qty="5">5</span> tbsp water
- **Lemon or lime juice** - a squeeze, to taste

## Equipment

- Stainless steel skillet
- Sharp knife
- Meat thermometer
- Pastry brush

---

## Method

1. **Score & Cure:** Score the duck fat in a diamond pattern, taking care not to cut into the meat. Season with salt and dry brine uncovered in the fridge for 6 hours.
2. **Prep for Sear:** Remove from fridge and pat *completely* dry with paper towels.
3. **Cold Start Render:** Place skin-side down in a cold stainless steel pan. Set heat to low-medium so the fat renders out slowly for around 10 minutes. Drain rendered fat as you go.
4. **Crisp & Finish:** Bump the heat up to crispen the skin. Flip and sear the flesh side until the internal temperature reaches **52-54°C**.
5. **Rest:** Transfer to a board and let rest for 10 min before slicing thinly on an angle.
6. **Pan Glaze:** Deglaze the pan with water, then whisk in hoisin sauce, soy sauce, rice vinegar, and a squeeze of fresh lemon or lime juice. Simmer until reduced to a glossy glaze.

> Bonus: put slices on top of rice to make duck nigiri
