## Ingredients

- <span data-qty="1000">1000</span>g plain flour
- <span data-qty="500">500</span>ml hot water (steaming, ~70–80°C)
- <span data-qty="8">8</span>tbsp sunflower oil (or light olive oil)
- <span data-qty="1.5">1.5</span>tsp fine sea salt - for the dough
- <span data-qty="1000">1000</span>g curd cheese (twaróg, quark, or well-drained cottage cheese)
- <span data-qty="2">2</span>medium eggs (or 2 egg yolks for a firmer filling)
- <span data-qty="10">10</span>tbsp poppy seeds - scalded and finely ground plain
- <span data-qty="1.5">1.5</span>tsp fine sea salt - for the filling (adjust to taste)
- <span data-qty="1">1</span>tsp freshly ground black pepper
- Fresh dill (1 large bunch, finely chopped) - makes big difference
- <span data-qty="4">4</span>tbsp sliced chives or spring onions - optional
- Butter or sour cream - for serving

## Method

1. **Scald and grind poppy seeds:** Pour boiling water over the poppy seeds, let steep for 10–15 minutes, drain thoroughly through a fine-mesh sieve, and grind in a mortar, blender, or coffee grinder until milky.

2. **Make the choux dough (заварне тісто):** In a large bowl or food processor bowl, combine <span data-qty="1000">1000</span>g plain flour and <span data-qty="1.5">1.5</span>tsp fine sea salt. Pour in <span data-qty="500">500</span>ml hot water (steaming, around 70–80°C) and <span data-qty="8">8</span>tbsp sunflower oil. Mix until a cohesive dough forms.

3. **Knead and rest:** Knead the dough on a lightly floured surface for 3–5 minutes until smooth and supple. Wrap tightly in cling film and let rest in the fridge for 20–30 minutes to relax the gluten.

4. **Prepare savoury filling:** In a medium bowl, mash the curd cheese with a fork. Add <span data-qty="2">2</span>medium eggs, the ground poppy seeds, <span data-qty="1.5">1.5</span>tsp fine sea salt, and <span data-qty="1">1</span>tsp black pepper. Fold in the fresh dill and sliced chives, then mix until evenly incorporated.

> Ensure the curd cheese is dry and well-drained before mixing; excess whey will make sealing the dumplings difficult.

5. **Roll and cut circles:** On a lightly floured worktop, roll out the rested dough to a thickness of 1–2 mm. Cut rounds using a drinking glass or round pastry cutter (~7–8 cm diameter).

6. **Assemble varenyky:** Place a generous teaspoon of savoury filling into the center of each circle. Fold the dough in half over the filling and pinch the edges firmly together to seal.

> At this point, flat freeze them for later: place each dumpling separated on a flour-dusted tray in the freezer. Once completely frozen, transfer them into freezer bags.

7. **Boil and serve:** Bring a large pot of salted water to a rolling boil. Drop the varenyky in batches, stir gently from the bottom to prevent sticking, and cook for 5 minutes once they float to the surface. Drain and toss immediately with butter or serve with cold sour cream, topped with fresh dill or chives.
