## Ingredients

- <span data-qty="500">500</span>g chicken breast
- <span data-qty="1">1</span> onion
- <span data-qty="1">1</span> jar (400g) chopped tomatoes
- <span data-qty="3">3</span> tbsp yoghurt
- Ghee - for searing
- Olive oil - a splash
- Salt and black pepper - to taste
- Spices of your choice (e.g., freshly ground coriander seed)
- Fresh greens of your choice (e.g., coriander), chopped

## Method

1. **Prep chicken:** Cut chicken breast into ~3cm chunks. Toss with salt, a bit of olive oil, and spices of your choice (freshly ground coriander seed works well).
2. **Preheat & dice:** Preheat pan, then add ghee. Dice the onion while it heats.
3. **Sear chicken:** Sear chicken over high heat with enough gaps between pieces so they brown nicely instead of steaming.
4. **Add onion:** Once browned, reduce heat to medium. Add the onion, a bit more salt, and black pepper; cook for a bit.
5. **Simmer with tomato:** Pour in the chopped tomatoes. Cook on medium-low until slightly thickened; break big chunks into smaller ones if you prefer or want it cooked faster.

> No need to cover with a lid - chicken breast is full of water, so it will still be juicy.

6. **Finish off heat:** Turn off the heat. Add yoghurt (around 3 tbsp per jar of chopped tomatoes) and chopped greens, then mix all.
