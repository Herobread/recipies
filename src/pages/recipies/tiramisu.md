## Ingredients

- <span data-qty="12">12</span>egg yolks
- <span data-qty="12">12</span>egg whites
- <span data-qty="100">100</span>g sugar - for the yolks
- <span data-qty="50">50</span>g sugar - for the meringue
- <span data-qty="1000">1000</span>g mascarpone
- <span data-qty="4">4</span>packs ladyfingers (savoiardi) - enough for 2 layers
- <span data-qty="500">500</span>ml strong brewed coffee - cooled
- <span data-qty="4">4</span>tbsp real aged balsamic (optional)
- Frozen dark chocolate bar (bitter, high cacao) - for grating

## Method

1. **Prep coffee:** Brew the coffee in advance and let it cool completely to room temperature. Mix in balsamic once fully cooled down (will evaporate if too hot).

2. **Zabaglione base:** In a large bowl, beat the yolks on high speed while gradually adding <span data-qty="100">100</span> g sugar until pale, thick, and doubled in volume.

3. **Incorporate mascarpone:** Add the mascarpone into the yolks. Mix fast first to break it down, then slowly and gently until smooth.

> Take care not to overwhip the mascarpone once added, or the cream can split and turn grainy.

4. **French meringue:** In a completely clean, grease-free bowl, whip the egg whites on high speed. Once foamy, gradually stream in the remaining <span data-qty="50">50</span> g sugar. Beat until stiff, glossy peaks form (it should hold firmly when inverted).

> Any trace of yolk or fat in the bowl or whisks will prevent the whites from reaching stiff peaks.

5. **Combine:** Gently fold the whipped whites into the mascarpone cream in three additions using a spatula, cutting down the center and sweeping around the bowl to preserve the air.

6. **Assemble:** Quickly dunk ladyfingers into the cooled coffee (a brief dip so they don't get too soggy) and arrange an even layer in your dish. Spread half the cream over top, add a second layer of soaked biscuits, and finish with the remaining cream.

> Grind frozen chocolate in a spice grinder (short pulses) or on a grater; it melts fast.

7. **Rest & serve:** Cover tightly with clingfilm and refrigerate for at least 6 hours, ideally 24-48 hours for the texture to set and flavors to mature. Grate chocolate over top before serving.
