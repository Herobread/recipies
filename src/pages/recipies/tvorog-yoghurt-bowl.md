## Method

1. **Mix the base:** Mash the tvorog, yogurt, chopped dill, salt, and black pepper together in a bowl until thick and creamy. Go light on salt if using salty toppings.
2. **Assemble:** Spread the tvorog mixture onto a shallow bowl or plate. Top with <recipe-selected for="protein" fallback="your protein"></recipe-selected> and <recipe-selected for="crunch" fallback="your crunch"></recipe-selected> right before eating.

## Serving Suggestion

Serve with thick-cut toasted sourdough, dark rye bread, or warm flatbread for scooping, finished with a light drizzle of olive oil or a squeeze of lemon juice.
