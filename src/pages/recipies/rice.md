## Ingredients

- <span data-qty="200">200</span>g long-grain rice (basmati or jasmine)
- <span data-qty="40">40</span>g raisins or sultanas
- Fine salt - to taste (aim for light sea water salinity)
- Water - plenty, at least 3-4x volume of rice

## Method

1. **Boil water:** Bring a large pot of water to a rolling boil over high heat. Season generously with salt until it tastes noticeably salty like seawater.

2. **Cook:** Add the rice and raisins directly to the boiling water. Maintain an active boil, stirring once or twice to prevent sticking, until the rice grains are tender with no chalky core remaining (typically 10-12 minutes).

3. **Drain:** Pour the contents of the pot through a fine-mesh colander to drain off all excess water thoroughly.

4. **Steam & rest:** Immediately return the hot, drained rice and raisins to the warm pot off the heat. Cover tightly with a lid and let rest undisturbed for 3-5 minutes.
