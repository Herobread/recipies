## Ingredients

- <span data-qty="300">300</span>g Flahavan's rolled jumbo oats
- <span data-qty="30">30</span>ml olive oil
- <span data-qty="1">1</span> large egg
- <span data-qty="80">80</span>g honey
- <span data-qty="50">50</span>g pumpkin seeds
- <span data-qty="50">50</span>g sunflower seeds
- <span data-qty="50">50</span>g flaked almonds
- <span data-qty="1">1</span> tsp ground cinnamon
- A pinch of Maldon sea salt flakes

## Method

1. **Preheat oven:** Preheat your oven to 150°C fan and line a large baking sheet with parchment paper.

2. **Whisk egg:** In a small bowl, whisk the egg until smooth and uniform. This binds the oats into chunky, extra-crispy clusters.

3. **Combine and mix:** In a large bowl, combine the Flahavan's rolled jumbo oats, pumpkin seeds, sunflower seeds, flaked almonds, cinnamon, and sea salt. Pour in the whisked egg, olive oil, and honey. Mix thoroughly until every oat is evenly coated in a thin sheen of oil and honey.
   > *Tip:* Adjust the honey to taste if you prefer a sweeter granola. Ensure everything is well-coated so the oats toast nicely rather than dry out.

4. **Bake:** Spread the granola in an even, slightly compact layer on the prepared baking sheet. Bake at 150°C fan for around 30 minutes, until golden brown.
   > *Note:* If you want large clusters, avoid stirring during baking.

5. **Cool completely:** Remove from the oven and allow to cool completely on the tray without disturbing it—the granola will crisp up as it cools. Once cooled, break into clusters and store in an airtight container.

## Serving Suggestion

Serve with Greek yoghurt/skyr, fresh berries or sliced fruit, and an extra drizzle of honey.
