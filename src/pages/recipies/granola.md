## Ingredients

- <span data-qty="300">300</span>g Flahavan's rolled jumbo oats
- <span data-qty="60">60</span>ml olive oil
- <span data-qty="80">80</span>g honey
- <span data-qty="50">50</span>g pumpkin seeds
- <span data-qty="50">50</span>g sunflower seeds
- <span data-qty="50">50</span>g almond flakes
- <span data-qty="1">1</span>tsp cinnamon
- Maldon sea salt flakes - a tiny bit

## Method

1. **Preheat oven:** Preheat your oven to 150°C fan and line a large baking sheet with parchment paper.

2. **Combine and mix:** In a large bowl, combine the Flahavan's rolled jumbo oats, pumpkin seeds, sunflower seeds, almond flakes, cinnamon, and a tiny bit of Maldon sea salt flakes. Pour in the olive oil and honey, then mix thoroughly until everything is evenly coated.

3. **Bake:** Spread the granola out into an even layer on the prepared baking sheet. Bake at 150°C fan for 30 minutes.

> Stir halfway through baking if you are cooking a lot or making a double batch to ensure an even toast.

4. **Cool completely:** Remove from the oven once nicely browned and let it cool down completely on the baking tray before handling or storing into airtight containers.
