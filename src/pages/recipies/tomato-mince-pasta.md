## Ingredients

- <span data-qty="300">300</span>g mince
- <span data-qty="3">3</span>g salt - 1% of mince weight
- <span data-qty="1">1</span>onion - finely chopped
- <span data-qty="400">400</span>g passata
- <span data-qty="200">200</span>g pasta of your choice
- Black pepper - to taste
- Wine - optional, a splash
- Balsamic vinegar - a splash, to finish
- Fresh herbs (e.g., basil) - to finish

## Method

1. **Season & shape:** Season the mince with <span data-qty="3">3</span>g salt (1% of its weight) and black pepper. Shape into large meatballs.

> Forming large meatballs leaves open pan space around them so they fry and brown nicely instead of steaming.

2. **Brown mince:** Sear the meatballs over high heat until well browned on the outside, then break them down and fry a bit more.
3. **Add onion:** Add the chopped onion and fry until softened.
4. **Deglaze & simmer:** Add a splash of wine if using and let it cook off, then pour in the passata. Simmer to reduce slightly.
5. **Boil pasta:** In parallel, boil your pasta of choice in salted water until al dente. Reserve some pasta water before draining.
6. **Combine & finish:** Add a splash of pasta water to the mince pan and reduce to your desired sauciness. Toss in the pasta, turn off the heat, and finish with a splash of balsamic vinegar and fresh herbs.
