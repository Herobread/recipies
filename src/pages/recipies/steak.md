## Ingredients

- <span data-qty="1">1</span>steak
- high-smoke-point fat (ghee, beef tallow, or avocado oil)
- Coarse flake salt - to finish
- Freshly cracked black pepper - to finish

## Method

1. **Preheat pan:** Place a heavy cast iron or carbon steel skillet over high heat, once almost at leidenfrost add fat, wait to see wisps of smoke appear. Fat should be good thickness, ie 1-3mm for the best sear.

> Use cookware with high thermal mass. Ie: aluminium < stainless steel < carbon steel < cast iron

1. **Sear & flip often:** Lay the meat into the hot fat. Flip every 30 to 45 seconds(as soon as it unsticks from pan) to build an even crust while keeping the heat from penetrating too deeply.

2. **Check core temp:** Use an instant-read probe and pull the meat from the pan 3-5°C below your final desired target temperature.

> Carryover cooking will continue to raise the internal temperature while resting off the heat.

4. **Rest & slice:** Transfer to a warm plate or wire rack and rest undisturbed for 5 to 8 minutes.
