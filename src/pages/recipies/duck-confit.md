## Ingredients

- <span data-qty="2">2</span> duck legs
- **Fine salt** - 1% of total meat weight
- **Coarse black pepper** - to taste
- <span data-qty="2">2</span> tsp duck fat, butter, or olive oil (split evenly across bags)
- <span data-qty="0.5">0.5</span> orange (zested)

## Equipment

- Blowtorch
- Vacuum sealer + <span data-qty="2">2</span> bags
- Sous vide circulator
- Oven with grill / broiler

---

## Method

1. **Prep the meat:** Remove any leftover feathers using a blowtorch. If washing with water, pat *completely* dry with paper towels.
2. **Season & Bag:** Weigh the legs to calculate salt (1% weight). Season all over with salt and coarse pepper. Drop each leg into a vacuum bag with half the fat and orange zest.
3. **Cure:** Vacuum seal tightly. Store in the fridge overnight (8–12 hours) to tenderize. Do not skip this step.
4. **Sous Vide:** Set water bath to **74°C**. Submerge the bags and cook for **8 to 12 hours**. (Pink meat is normal).
5. **Chill:** Shock in an ice bath or cold water sink for 10 minutes until no longer warm.

> **Storage note:** At this point, the sealed bags keep for **1 week in the fridge** or **3 months in the freezer**.

6. **Finish & Crisp:** Pat the skin bone-dry. Roast under a hot oven grill skin-side up until deeply golden and glass-crispy (internal temp around 40–70°C).
