## Ingredients

- <span data-qty="300">300</span>g type 00 flour (low protein, ~9%)
- <span data-qty="150">150</span>g granulated sugar
- <span data-qty="150">150</span>g unsalted butter - softened to room temperature
- <span data-qty="15">15</span>g extra virgin olive oil
- <span data-qty="1">1</span>whole egg
- <span data-qty="0.5">0.5</span>tsp fine salt
- <span data-qty="1">1</span>tsp vanilla extract
- Finely grated zest of 1 lemon

## Method

1. **Cream fats & sugar:** In a mixing bowl, beat the room-temperature butter, olive oil, and sugar together until pale and smooth.

2. **Add wet ingredients:** Whisk in the egg, vanilla extract, and lemon zest until fully emulsified into the creamed butter.

3. **Incorporate dry goods:** Sift the flour and salt directly into the bowl. Mix by hand just until the dough comes together into a cohesive ball.

> Do not overwork the dough; excess gluten development will ruin the characteristic short, crumbly texture.

4. **Roll & chill:** If the dough is firm and workable, roll between two sheets of baking paper to a uniform thickness of 5 mm. If greasy or too soft, refrigerate for 20-30 minutes before rolling.

5. **Shape:** Using a sharp knife or fluted pastry wheel, cut the chilled sheet into pointed oval / lemon shapes (approximately 9 cm long by 4.5 cm wide). Pinch the tapered tips lightly to define the classic silhouette.

> Cut a template from parchment paper or card stock to keep sizing consistent across the batch.

6. **Final chill:** Place the cut shapes on a parchment-lined baking tray and refrigerate until firm to the touch before baking.

> Chilling prevents the butter and olive oil from melting out prematurely in the oven, keeping the defined edges sharp.

7. **Bake:** Bake in an oven preheated to 170°C (fan) for about 20 minutes, or until evenly golden.

8. **Cool & set:** Leave the biscuits to cool completely on the baking tray. They remain fragile while warm and solidify to their signature crisp snap as they reach room temperature.
