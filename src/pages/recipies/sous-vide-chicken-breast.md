## Ingredients

- <span data-qty="2">2</span>boneless, skinless chicken breasts
- <span data-qty="1">1</span>tbsp olive oil or neutral oil
- Fine salt - 1% of total meat weight
- Black pepper, garlic powder, or dry herbs - to taste

## Method

1. **Season & bag:** Pat chicken dry. Season evenly with salt, oil, and dry spices. Place into a vacuum bag and seal tightly.

> Avoid fresh raw garlic or delicate fresh herbs in sous vide bags; powders and dried spices distribute better and do not develop bitter off-flavors at low heat.

2. **Sous vide:** Submerge the bag in a water bath preheated to 64°C and cook for 1 to 2 hours.

> 64°C keeps the breast juicy and tender without the chalky texture of overcooked poultry. Do not exceed 4 hours or the texture will turn mushy.

3. **Finish:** Remove from bag and pat completely dry. Slice directly for salads or sear in a screaming hot pan with oil for 45-60 seconds per side for color.
